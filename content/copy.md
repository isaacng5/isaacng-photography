## About
Isaac Ng, an amateur photographer from Hong Kong, has found his passion for capturing life's moments in the bustling city of London. During his spare time, he delves into the world of photography, using his Nikon Z6ii as his creative tool of choice.

With an open-minded approach to photography, Isaac embraces a wide range of subjects, from expressive portraits to mesmerising landscapes and the enchanting world of animals. His lens becomes a window into the diverse and multifaceted aspects of life, seeking out the hidden beauties that often go unnoticed.

Rather than viewing photography as a full-time pursuit, Isaac values it as a cherished outlet for creative expression and personal enjoyment. He takes pictures solely when inspiration strikes, allowing each shot to unfold organically and authentically, free from the constraints of forced obligations.

Isaac's keen eye for detail and sensitivity to colours lend a distinctive aura to his photographs, inviting viewers to experience the world through his unique perspective. Join him on this artistic odyssey as he uncovers the beauty within the mundane and embraces the ever-changing nature of his photographic exploration.
